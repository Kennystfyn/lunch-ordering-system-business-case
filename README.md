# Canteen Ordering System — Business Analysis Capstone Project

**Company (case study):** Unilever UK Office
**Project type:** Business Analysis Capstone — full project lifecycle
**Methodology:** Agile (Scrum, Jira — 3 sprints, 2-week cycles)

## Background
Unilever's UK office has ~1,500 employees across 12 floors, served by just 2 canteens seating ~150 people each (300 total). Most employees eat between 12pm and 1pm, causing heavy congestion — management observed employees spending ~60 minutes on the lunch round-trip but only 10–15 minutes actually eating. The canteen also lacked demand visibility: popular dishes sold out while other meals were thrown away unsold.

## Business problem
Long queues, overcrowding, and lost productive time during the lunch rush, combined with poor demand planning that caused both food shortages and food wastage.

## Approach
Delivered the full BA lifecycle for this capstone:
- **Business case** — problem statement, objectives, 3 options considered (do nothing / expand canteen capacity / implement an ordering system), recommended option, and full cost-benefit analysis
- **Stakeholder analysis matrix** — 10 stakeholder groups scored by influence and impact, from Senior Management down to Delivery staff
- **RACI matrix** — 17 project activities mapped across 10 roles
- **43 functional requirements** (FR001–FR043), prioritized High/Medium
- **AS-IS and TO-BE process maps** — current manual queue-and-serve process vs. the future online-order-and-deliver process, both swimlaned by role
- **User stories & acceptance criteria** — 17 stories covering employees, Menu Manager, Canteen Manager, Chef, Delivery, Payroll, and Admin
- **Product backlog and 3-sprint plan** in Jira (Sprint 1: menu & ordering, Sprint 2: checkout & prep, Sprint 3: delivery, feedback, payroll & access control)

## Recommended solution
A web-based Canteen Ordering System letting employees order lunch online before an 11am cut-off and receive it delivered to their workstation — with menu management, an order dashboard for the canteen team, delivery tracking, feedback capture, and a monthly payroll deduction report.

## Financial analysis
- **Total estimated project cost:** £140,000 (development, testing/deployment, payroll integration, training, first-year support, contingency)
- **Total estimated annual benefit:** £1,680,000 (£1.65M in productivity savings from ~900 employees saving ~20 min/day, plus £30K from reduced food waste)
- **Net annual benefit:** £1,540,000
- **Payback period:** ~1 month (0.08 years)
- **ROI: 1,100%**

## Success metrics defined
≥50% reduction in average lunch waiting time · ≥60% employee adoption within 3 months · ≥95% order delivery accuracy · ≥90% on-time delivery · ≥30% food wastage reduction · ≥80% employee satisfaction · ≥98% payroll deduction accuracy
